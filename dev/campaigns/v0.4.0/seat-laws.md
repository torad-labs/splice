24. A MEASUREMENT IS TWO CLAIMS, AND EACH HAS ITS OWN FAILURE (2026-09-18, from the MCP re-pricing, where three seats were wrong in three different directions on one number in one afternoon). 35. THE VERB THAT PRINTS EVERY ROW IS THE ONE WHERE TWO IMPLEMENTATIONS CAN DISAGREE (2026-09-18, splice-builder, four for four across the V4-142 conversions). A checker usually has several modes and the gate calls only some of them. The ones the gate calls — check, and a selftest — print on FAILURE, so on a clean tree they reach no state where a formatting or ordering difference can show: two implementations agree because neither prints anything interesting. The census verb (report, --packages, --list) prints EVERY ROW, and that is where a port's real differences surface. Measured here on quirks-keys-documented: check and --selftest were byte-identical while report was wrong in every line, because the original's format spec bound to the LAST expression before it, so `{klass}.{prop:22s}` padded prop ALONE and padding the concatenation shifted every column by the length of the class name. The gate never calls report, so nothing in the ladder would ever have caught it. SO: differential EVERY mode a checker supports, including the ones the gate does not run and especially the one that enumerates — and treat agreement on the failure-only verbs as no evidence at all, the same way law 27 treats two invocations that both exit 0. The orchestrator ran two of four modes on this file and was lucky rather than careful; the builder ran four and found it.
