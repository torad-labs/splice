## Module: explainer

- One idea, built in steps. Say the conclusion in one line at the top, then step through it.
- Steps: a Back and a Next button over a small panel per step, each panel a sentence and a picture, with the step's
  number shown. The first step is visible without any script.
- When the idea is about how something responds, let the person move one input, such as a slider over a parameter, and
  redraw the picture from it as they move.
- Keep the state in a few variables and draw everything from them, so every control stays in step with the picture.
- Short text per step. A paragraph belongs in the reply, not in the frame.
