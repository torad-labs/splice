# LAW [2026-09-18]: THE BUG IS USUALLY IN THE SHAPE OF THE CHECK, NOT THE SHAPE OF THE FIX (2026-09-18, from the MCP containment exchange with hostshield-orchestrator; it generalises every did-not-run this campaign has found).
